Associate · 2025–Present

Previous Standards Work

W3C Device & Sensors Working Group

Invited Expert · 2018–2025

I spent seven years working on the privacy and security threat models for hardware-level Web APIs. This work connected my academic research on sensor vulnerabilities with platform-level mitigations for fingerprinting and unauthorised sensor access.

Standards Impact

My earlier security research contributed to changes in browser security and W3C specifications, including pressure-sensor standardisation and privacy considerations for the Motion/Orientation APIs.

I also co-chair the Emerging Trends in Web Advertising workshop at The Web Conference 2026, bringing together research and practice around privacy-preserving advertising, AI, immersive Web experiences, and the future of Web advertising. The findings directly inform web standardisation.

This is the part of my career where research, implementation, and standards meet: identifying a technical problem, understanding its implications at Web scale, and helping turn that understanding into a platform-level constraint or capability.
